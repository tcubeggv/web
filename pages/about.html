About TCube

Who We Are

The Entrepreneurship Cell of Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya, Bilaspur.

Our Aim

We want entrepreneurship to feel accessible to every student on campus — not just those who already have a startup idea in mind. GGV should be a place where testing an idea, building something small, and learning by doing feels like a normal thing to try.

Our Motivation

Most student ideas never leave the WhatsApp group they were born in — not for lack of merit, but for lack of a next step. TCube is that next step: a team and a community that turns a rough idea into a real pitch, a real product, or a real team.

What We Do

Four things, mainly:

01

Ideation Support

Workshops and one-on-one sessions to sharpen a raw idea into something pitchable.

02

Events & Hackathons

Ideathons, bootcamps, and pitch days that give ideas a real deadline and a real audience.

03

Mentorship

Connecting student founders with alumni and mentors who've built things before.

04

Networking & Access

Building a campus-wide founder community, and pointing teams toward funding and resources.
